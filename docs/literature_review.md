# Targeted literature review

Verified against primary sources on 2026-10-14. This is a focused landscape review, not an exhaustive novelty search. “Not extracted” means a comparable numerical compute budget was not established from the inspected source; it does not mean the original paper has no efficiency analysis. Paper-reported gains are not reproduced here.

## Closest prior art

**Search-Adaptor** is an essential frozen-encoder precedent. Its published method adapts both query and corpus embeddings using a shared residual network. Our query-only deterministic MLP is therefore a baseline inspired by adaptation, not a reproduction of Search-Adaptor. A frozen encoder does not necessarily mean an unchanged document index.

**RELER**, submitted October 6, 2026, directly overlaps with reinforcement learning of retrieval actions in embedding space. The arXiv record inspected lists a preprint and no accepted venue. It samples directional embeddings, updates encoders using RLOO, and includes a fixed-document-index RAG setting with query-encoder adaptation. This project must not claim to introduce embedding-space retrieval reinforcement learning. Its narrower experimental question concerns small query-conditioned adapters, frozen query and document encoders, Gaussian versus mixture policies, and explicit retrieval-cost controls.

## Implications for this implementation

Established ingredients include frozen-embedding adaptation, conditional mixtures, multi-vector representations, stochastic embeddings, score-function estimators, and retrieval-reward optimization. Their combination is an experiment, not evidence of novelty.

The tractable policy density here is on **raw Euclidean actions**. Normalizing a Gaussian sample produces a directional distribution; the raw Gaussian log density must not be described as a spherical density. Score-function optimization is nevertheless valid when normalized retrieval is part of the reward function on the raw action.

A distribution over locations induces a distribution over documents through nearest-neighbor cells. Large-volume cells can attract probability mass without reflecting semantic uncertainty. Mixture weights and variance alone do not establish ambiguity, calibration, or useful modes. Inspect sampled top-1 document occupancy, relevant cluster coverage, and distances in the original space alongside projections.

Surrogate training averages each stochastic location's loss and can favor a compromise region. Our deterministic multi-head surrogate instead uses document-wise max aggregation, so it can specialize heads. This objective difference is explicit and means a win cannot be attributed exclusively to probabilistic versus deterministic representation. Future matched-parameter and matched-objective studies are required.

## Remaining review work

Before a paper-level claim, fully extract pEBR's objective and encoder behavior, reproduce a stronger query-only Search-Adaptor variant, check RELER revisions and author implementation, and collect comparable numerical resource reports. No claim is made that this review exhausts 2026 literature.

| [NeuRoute, August 2026 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.15438) | Lightweight neural binary addresses, uncertainty-guided bucket probing and local clustering | Fixed vector search with learned hashing; logit-guided probing | Abstract reports sub-hour training/construction on billion-scale datasets; not reproduced here. Region classification, probabilistic routing, and learned partition selection are established ideas. The useful question here is whether a small relevance-trained router improves the quality/pruning tradeoff over frozen BGE geometry on held-out questions. A positive result would need validation beyond one small question-answer benchmark.
